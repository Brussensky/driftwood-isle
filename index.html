How to play: tap an item to pick it up, tap a matching item to merge. Seed + seed = sprout, sprout + sprout = palm, palm + palm = coins. Tap an empty tile to move, tap Collect for new seeds, spend coins in the Shop.

Sonic identity

Soft plucked strings, glassy tones and wooden knocks over a relaxed groove: a sound you can leave on for an hour. Every tonal sound sits in F major, mostly on the pentatonic notes F G A C D, so overlapping sounds always agree with each other and with the music.

Feedback grows with progress. Each merge tier adds notes, range and layers, and sits 2 LU louder than the one below: tier 3 runs over two octaves, adds a low body and air, and lands on a soft Fmaj7 before the coins pay out. Frequent sounds come in three variations, and the engine adds a random ±2% pitch on top, so taps and drops stay pleasant after thousands of plays.

Merges in a row build a combo. Each merge within 3 seconds of the last adds a step whose top note climbs C5, F5, A5, D6, F6, one LU louder each time, with a low C joining on the final step. Pause and the ladder resets. Every sound was also checked through a 300 Hz high-pass to make sure it still reads on a phone speaker.

  • 48 kHz / 24-bit masters
  • Key: F major pentatonic
  • Loop: 85 BPM, 4 bars, seamless
  • Loudness matched by momentary max
  • Music ducks −6 dB under the stinger
  • Combo ladder: 5 steps, 3 s window
  • Phone-speaker check: 300 Hz high-pass
